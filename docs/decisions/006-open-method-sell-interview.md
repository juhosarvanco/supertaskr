# ADR-006: Open-source the method; sell the planning phase; keep the factory internal

Date: 2026-08-14 · Status: accepted · Decided in: planning chat; later confirmed by market research

## Context
Solo founder, four active product threads, platform risk in the
orchestration layer. Market later confirmed: boards are a free
knife-fight with a graveyard; the planning layer monetizes (ChatPRD).

## Decision
Three layers: (1) method/ open source as distribution and credibility;
(2) the guided planning phase as the paid product if pull emerges —
evidence-first: 2–3 manual interviews with real people before building
it; (3) the full multi-model loop primarily as internal capability.

## Consequences
Launch post = method + this repo as living demo. Dashboard is
marketing that happens to be useful, not the revenue line. Revisit
after milestone 1 ships and the manual-interview test runs.
