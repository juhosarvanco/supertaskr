## What was measured

@human ruled universality a v1 acceptance criterion on 2026-09-10. A design claim becomes a measured one only on a second project; the language is @human's to choose and the card records it when chosen.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN the genesis runs for the second project THE five documents, the cards directory, the runtime template, the hooks and the gates SHALL exist in it with no edit by hand, and the interview's answers SHALL be the only input.
- WHEN one card is dispatched in it THE arm SHALL cut the lane, the executor SHALL build and run the project's own suites through the same runner, the verifier SHALL judge, the arm SHALL merge, the push SHALL owe its range and CI SHALL read green — each step's exit recorded in the card, and the cycle time and tokens read into the bands.
- IF any step needs a change to this repository THEN the change SHALL be a card here naming the layout assumption, and the second project's card SHALL wait on it — never a hand fix in the second project.
