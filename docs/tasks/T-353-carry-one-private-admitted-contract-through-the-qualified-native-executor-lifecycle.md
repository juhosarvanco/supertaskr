An approved native executor works in its explicitly owned product and private-records checkouts under one frozen private contract. Every supported action checks every participating root, and successful collection retains each exact candidate before the complete owned set is released. The product checkout need not contain a task card. This is executor plumbing; paired verification, split delivery and default/operator activation remain later consumers.

## Finding and reconciliation

The accepted T-352 source at 42d20fe611d02d8a352a76f28973fa9da58987ea supplies read-only private approval preview, not native execution authority. It is locally merged at 2d47fe70a65e4b8d985982c5dba99e1598eca61a; final publication and exact-tip CI must pass before this card dispatches. This card joins those existing boundaries through the normal lifecycle without a second approval policy or ownership ledger.
