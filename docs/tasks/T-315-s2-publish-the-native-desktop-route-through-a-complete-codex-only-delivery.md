The fresh verifier SHALL receive the frozen candidate and required sealed inputs without executor conversation inheritance. Small controls SHALL demonstrate writer reservation conflict, unknown attribution or refused concurrent writer, out-of-fence hold, and interruption that remains non-terminal until owned jobs are reconciled. A hook refusal alone is not a passed stop control.
- **Close and handoff.** The seat SHALL release only after all native attempts and jobs are reconciled. The durable handoff SHALL distinguish implementation, verification, local merge, push and CI, and state the exact native capabilities demonstrated. The first delivery SHALL not claim parallel writers, arbitrary tool coverage, filesystem/read isolation, transparent recovery after parent loss or any unexercised task class.
