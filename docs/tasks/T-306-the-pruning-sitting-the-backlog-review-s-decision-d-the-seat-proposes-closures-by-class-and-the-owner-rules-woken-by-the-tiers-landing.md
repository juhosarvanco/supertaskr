## What was measured

At the loop sitting the board held about 690 cards, 129 of them parked and around 80 flagged as stating no wake condition at all; the triage clusters (T-282) and the wake field (T-285) make the pass mechanical, and the tiers (T-296) change what a card costs, so the closures are judged against the process being moved to, not the one left. A closed card stays a record with its reason; nothing is deleted.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN this card wakes THE seat SHALL derive the list from the board (the triage clusters, the parked cards' wake conditions, the cards whose fence or subject a later card overtook) and propose, class by class, one of three dispositions per card — closed as overtaken with the card that overtook it named, merged into a class parent with its criteria kept whole, or kept with a wake condition written — and the owner SHALL rule on the list before any card moves.
- WHEN a card is closed THE record SHALL carry the disposition and the reason appended, its status a legal one, and the triage band SHALL be re-read after the sitting.
- WHEN the sitting ends THE checkpoint SHALL carry the counts before and after by class.
