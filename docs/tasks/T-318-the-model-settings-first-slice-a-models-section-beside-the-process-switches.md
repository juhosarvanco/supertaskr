## What was measured

At ee9ed3cd the runtime template's `roles:` block names one `model@session` string per role (planner, builder, verifier, integrator, janitor), all `claude-opus-5@subagent`, and no entry for the coordinator; `session_policy` names the default session mode. The dispatch plan in the arm resolves a builder default and a verifier default from the template's models, applies the executor and verifier overrides passed at dispatch, and builds the stamp from them, refusing an absent default before the ritual stamps a card or cuts a worktree; the plan knows no effort, no provider and no harness. The model configuration carries no effort field; run records (T-311) already carry harness, model and effort per assignment as receipts. The process section names a profile, the offered profiles and departures, read by the parser library's process settings module exported through the library's browser entry (T-317) and rendered by the terminal (T-300, T-300-s6), the app (T-301, dormant) and the skill (T-302, planned). No models table, no provider or account filter, no harness capabilities table and no designated-flagship list exist. The room's rulings of 2026-09-13 fix the controls, the names, Economy's policy, the precedence, the effort semantics, the placement and this slice's scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN the schema declares the models section THE rows SHALL be the seats the loop dispatches (coordinator, planner, phase-one verifier, executor, phase-two verifier, integrator, janitor); the columns SHALL be the built-in profiles Economy, Balanced and Quality and any named user profile, each column carrying a revision identifier and a review date, and Diverse SHALL be an optional allocation variation a column declares naming which seats differ and why, curated and labelled as curated; each cell SHALL name a harness, a provider, a model identifier valid for that harness and an effort value; a `models:` list SHALL classify every model the section may name as flagship or non-flagship explicitly, with pinned versions preferred where available and any identifier whose underlying version can change identified as such, and no dated identifier SHALL ever be manufactured; a `capabilities:` table SHALL declare per harness and model the supported effort stops or that the model has no effort control; a `providers:` filter SHALL name the allowed providers and accounts; and the parser library's module, exported through its browser entry, SHALL refuse a missing seat, a harness or provider not declared, a model absent from the classification list, an effort outside the model's declared stops, a profile without a revision identifier and review date, and an Economy column giving a flagship-classified model to any seat but the coordinator.
- WHEN the template names a selected profile, project defaults, tier defaults, explicit role settings, the shared team-effort stop and a dispatch override THE resolver SHALL resolve each seat's harness, provider, model and effort in the order profile, project defaults, tier defaults, explicit role settings, dispatch override, an explicit role pin beating a tier default; the shared team-effort stop SHALL supply the default effort mapping to each model's concrete supported level and SHALL be overridden by tier settings, explicit role pins and dispatch overrides in that same precedence, so a pinned role stays pinned when the shared dial changes, pinned by a body; the resolver SHALL record per value the layer it came from; changing only effort SHALL leave every selected model unchanged, pinned by a body; a model without an effort control SHALL resolve to "no effort control" and never to an invented level; and the resolver SHALL then validate the RESOLVED assignment, nested workers included, against Economy's policy, the provider filter and the supplied constraints, refusing with the conflict named rather than changing any value.
- WHEN a resolved assignment departs from the selected Economy column THE resolver SHALL distinguish an ordinary customization that still satisfies Economy's policy from a departure that breaks it; a breaking departure SHALL resolve only when the template records its authorization explicitly and SHALL be displayed as "Economy — modified" naming the departure; the display SHALL never stand in for the authorization.
- WHEN the template supplies budget and eligibility constraints THE constraints SHALL be explicit values per provider and account (a limit or "unknown", and whether the limit is enforced or advisory); an advisory constraint with unknown availability SHALL stay visibly unknown, and an enforced constraint whose availability cannot be established SHALL refuse the authorization with the reason; the preview SHALL spend and reserve nothing; the dispatch plan SHALL check, before creating any side effect, the applicable authorization and the prerequisites and demonstrated capabilities required by each assignment's role and launch mechanism (a CLI child and a natively spawned child are different mechanisms, and a demonstration for one role establishes nothing for another), refusing a missing capability by name; and no subscription telemetry SHALL be built by this card.
- WHEN the terminal previews the assignments THE command SHALL list, per seat, the resolved harness, provider and model, the concrete supported effort beside the requested preference, the layer each value came from, and every departure and refusal with its reason; an unsupported explicit pin SHALL be refused with an actionable message naming the supported stops; the command's four exit codes SHALL stay unchanged; and the reference SHALL carry the models section, regenerated by the same generator.
- WHEN the dispatch arm plans a lane THE plan SHALL take the executor's and both verifiers' harness, provider, model and effort from this resolver's resolved assignment and from nothing else, SHALL refuse an invalid assignment before the ritual stamps a card or cuts a worktree, SHALL keep the card's existing `builder:` and `verifier:` provenance fields in their `model@session` syntax, and SHALL write the complete assignments (per seat: harness, provider, model, effort and the layer each value came from, effort kept apart from session identity) into a structured dispatch snapshot the arm writes into the lane's runtime directory beside its fence manifest, hashed and named in the stamp commit; a body SHALL show the snapshot, the stamp's fields and the brief's model row agreeing with the preview for the same seat under the same template, and a second body SHALL show an invalid assignment refused with no worktree, no snapshot and no stamp created. Writing the resolved assignment into the run record's receipt is follow-up work named in the notes, not this card's. Launching a child with the resolved harness, model and effort is the adapters' boundary (T-312, T-316) and is named in the notes, not claimed by this card.
- WHEN this card lands THE template SHALL be migrated with its current values preserved by default: planner unchanged; builder becomes executor; the verifier's value becomes both the phase-one and the phase-two verifier's, each a fresh session; integrator and janitor unchanged; `session_policy` unchanged; the coordinator row added with the selection the owner supplies explicitly for this card, named in the notes and in the template, never derived from taking the seat; the seat verbs SHALL record the running seat's observed model separately, `unknown` when unavailable, and taking the seat SHALL rewrite no profile; the selected profile SHALL name these values so approval activates no new default, and any change that would implement the standing operating policy beyond them SHALL be identified explicitly in the notes and left to the owner; the requested identity and the observed identity SHALL be kept apart wherever both are recorded, unavailable details recorded as unknown, and the run record's receipt integration SHALL be named as follow-up; the method version SHALL bump with its release note and evaluation block; and the app's screen (T-301) and the skill (T-302) SHALL be named in the notes as the remaining renderers of the models section, each its own card.

## Amendment of 2026-09-13 — account binding (proposed by the Codex orchestrator's queue review of 2026-09-13, approved by the owner on 2026-09-13)

Amendment proposed 2026-09-13 — account binding. Each resolved assignment identifies the configured account against which its eligibility and budget constraints are evaluated. The account comes from an explicit configured selection or the single eligible account; an ambiguous selection is refused by name, and an unavailable observation remains unknown under the card's existing enforced-versus-advisory rules. The preview and dispatch snapshot include that account identifier and its selection provenance. No credentials are stored and no login, account or authentication route is switched by this card. Bodies cover two accounts at one provider with different constraints, an explicit selection, and an ambiguous unselected case; no budget is pooled and no second account is substituted to make an assignment pass.

## Amendment of 2026-09-13 — observed seat reporting (proposed by the Codex orchestrator's queue review of 2026-09-13, approved by the owner on 2026-09-13)

Amendment proposed 2026-09-13 — observed seat reporting. The fence additionally grants tools/e2e/scripts/brief.mjs for the seat-command reporting required by the final criterion. The observation is emitted separately from the configured model and checkout-ownership identity; unavailable runtime evidence is reported as unknown, never inferred from the chosen profile or a process name. Taking or releasing the seat changes no model selection. This criterion requires no holder-record schema change and no run-record receipt integration beyond the follow-up already named. A body preserves the configured selection while the observed value is different or unknown.
