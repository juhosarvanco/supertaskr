review badge — the honesty trio
✓independent — solid disc ✓same-model — solid disc, same mark self-verified — half disc
A filled disc means someone actually checked the work; half-fill means the builder signed off on itself, and reads visibly weaker at a glance. Independent vs same-model is carried by the label and the detail panel rather than the mark.

04 · additions — name, purpose
tokenpurpose
--status-*-borderEach status needs a border a step darker than its fill — flat cards on a flat canvas need an edge, and in dark mode the fill alone is nearly invisible.
--column-header-bg / -fg / -idFeature headers invert between schemes (ink pill on white, bone pill on black) — that inversion can't come from --primary alone once themes exist.
--hairlineThe 1px rules under headers, the milestone slice, and the column footer. Quieter than --border, which stays for controls.
--ghost-borderDashed edge for suggestion cards — an idea, not a commitment. Never gets a fill.
--shadow-card / --shadow-popElevation, per scheme: hairline lift in light, none in dark (dark uses border only); --shadow-pop for the detail drawer, tooltips, popovers.
--review-disc / --review-markProvenance is its own semantic, not a reuse of --status-done-fg. One disc colour and one mark: full disc = checked, half disc = self-verified.
--drift-bg / --drift-fgThe warning semantic for drift and contradicted premises. Deliberately violet, not amber — both ambers are already spoken for by building and verifying, so "amber" would have been unreadable next to them.

06 · map tokens — architecture map
The map adds no status colours. It adds one semantic — --warning, for drift and, later, truth maintenance — plus the canvas chrome. The rule that keeps drift amber and building amber apart is structural, not chromatic: status is always a fill, warning is always a stroke. --warning never becomes a background, so the two ambers are never the same kind of thing on screen. Chroma does the rest: status fills sit at ~0.03, warning at ~0.13.

tokenwhere it earns its place
--warningDrift ring on nodes, drift edges, the finding ids in the panel. AA on both canvases at 1.5px. Never a fill — that is the whole separation from building amber.
--map-canvas / --map-gridOne step off the app background so the pane reads as a surface you pan, not a page. Grid may be set transparent without breaking anything.
--map-node-border-selectedInk in light, bone in dark. Selection is the one place chrome outranks fill, so it inverts rather than tints.
--map-edge-plannedDotted, quieter than confirmed. Early in a build most of the map is planned; it must read as normal, never as missing.
--map-unmapped-* / --map-group-bgGhost-card family at map scale: dashed border, no fill weight, muted ink. Same vocabulary as suggested cards on the board.
